Florida Wetland Photography

Morning Stillness

A heron stands quietly in a Florida wetland as colorful dawn light spreads across the sky and reflects in the calm water below.

Morning Stillness is built around the contrast between the small, solitary bird and the larger landscape — a peaceful early-morning scene shaped by soft light, open water, and quiet atmosphere.

About the Photograph

Quiet light over a Florida wetland

The photograph emphasizes atmosphere rather than a tight wildlife portrait. The heron becomes part of the wider wetland scene, surrounded by open water, distant vegetation, and the changing colors of morning.

It is part of my collection of Florida wildlife and nature photography focused on natural light, wetlands, and quiet moments in the landscape.
